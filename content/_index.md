Cominvent AS is the independent consulting practice of **Jan Høydahl** — based in
Drammen, Norway, working with clients worldwide.

Jan has worked in IT since **1995** and has spent well over a decade at the heart
of open-source search. He is a **member of the Apache Software Foundation** and a
**committer and PMC member on Apache Lucene and Apache Solr** — one of the people
who build and steer the technology itself — with deep expertise in both **Apache
Solr** and **OpenSearch**.

As a **sole consultant**, you work directly with Jan: no account managers, no
hand-offs. Beyond search, Cominvent brings the same rigour to **AI & chatbots**,
**open-source data & lakehouse** platforms and **cyber security** — designing,
building and hardening the systems, and handing over the knowledge so your team
can run them.
