Cominvent AS is the independent consulting practice of **Jan Høydahl** — based in
Drammen, Norway, working with clients worldwide.

Jan has worked in IT since **1995**, starting at **Ericsson** and then at search
pioneer **FAST Search & Transfer** before going independent through Cominvent. For
well over a decade he has been at the heart of open-source search: a **member of
the Apache Software Foundation**, an **Apache Lucene &amp; Solr committer and PMC
member**, and a past **Chair of the Apache Solr project** — one of the people who
build and steer the technology itself. His search work spans **Apache Solr,
Lucene, OpenSearch and Elasticsearch**.

As a **sole consultant**, you work directly with Jan: no account managers, no
hand-offs. Beyond search, Cominvent brings the same rigour to **AI & chatbots**,
**open-source data & lakehouse** platforms and **cyber security** — designing,
building and hardening the systems, and handing over the knowledge so your team
can run them.
