Cominvent AS is an independent consultancy based in Oslo, Norway, working with
clients internationally. For years we've been a go-to name in open-source
**Apache Solr** and search — and today we bring that same depth to **AI &
chatbots**, **open-source data & lakehouse** platforms and **cyber security**.

We're a small team of senior specialists: you work directly with the people
doing the engineering, not a layer of account managers. Whether it's tuning
search relevance, standing up a RAG chatbot, designing a lakehouse or hardening
the systems that run them, we help you build things that work — and hand over
the knowledge so your team can run them.
